Personal Hearing Test

Song Enhancer · about 2 minutes

Everyone hears differently, especially high frequencies. This test measures each ear so the enhancer can compensate for your hearing.

  1. Use the headphones you listen with, in a quiet room.
  2. Set your computer volume to your normal music level and leave it there.
  3. For each tone, drag the slider down until you can just barely hear it, then press Next.

This is a listening-comfort tool, not a medical hearing test.

quieterlouder

Your hearing profile is saved and active. The enhancer now gently boosts the frequencies you hear less well (up to +10 dB).
